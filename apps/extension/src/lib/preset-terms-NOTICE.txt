Preset terminology sources

Shopify Standard Product Taxonomy
https://github.com/Shopify/product-taxonomy/tree/cd90d2796a6cab4a8975ebb4e9832354752ee525/data/localizations/values
The attribute identifiers above individual records in preset-terms.js identify
the selected values. They cover fabrics, abrasives, metals, polymers, filter
media and fasteners. Huaci adapted attribute values into standalone terms,
including ripstop nylon, HEPA filter and activated carbon filter. The five
entries marked project/ are additional textile and leather concepts selected
by Huaci. Translations, aliases and context rules have been adapted or added
by the project, including all Arabic renderings.
