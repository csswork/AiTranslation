Select, right-click, and keep reading. Read whole articles side by side with their translation, and translate selected text, images and PDFs right where they are—without switching tabs or copying and pasting.

Huaci – AI Translator & Bilingual Reader is an open-source browser extension built on Manifest V3. Start translating right after installation with key-free credits, or use your own OpenAI, DeepSeek or DeepL API key.

▍Bilingual reading (new in 2.2.0)

Right-click anywhere on a page without selecting text and choose “Read this article bilingually”. A light sweeps across the page as the title and body are detected, then a clean reading page opens next to it with each original paragraph aligned beside its translation. Navigation, sidebars, ad slots and recommendation widgets are left out and never sent for translation.

• Side by side, every paragraph pair starts at the same height; you can also stack them, or show only the translation or only the original
• Light, dark or follow-system appearance, plus adjustable color palette, font, size, line height and page width—all remembered
• “Save” keeps the article and its translation on your device; open it again from the list on the right without translating it again, or delete it when you no longer need it

▍Click a word for another wording—and keep your preferred terms

On the reading page, in the selection popup, in the translation panel and on the PDF page, click a word in the translation—or drag over a few words—to see several alternative renderings with short notes, or type your own. Replace the same word elsewhere in one go.

Wordings you choose are remembered on your device as wording preferences: later translations of text that contains the same source term follow your choice. Review and delete them in Settings.

▍Six ways to translate, all in the right-click menu

• Selected text
Select text and choose “Translate to…” from the right-click menu. The translation appears in a centered popup, word by word where the provider supports streaming. You can also use the floating button next to a selection, or the shortcut ⌘⇧Y on macOS / Alt+Shift+T elsewhere. The popup can be dragged, shows the original on demand, and translates the original again when you switch the target language.

• Images
Right-click an image to recognize its text. Each passage is outlined along its strokes, and the translation is placed back over the original without the passages covering each other. Vertical Japanese text, as in manga, stays vertical when translated into Chinese, Japanese or Korean. Review the detected shapes first—move, edit, delete or draw new ones—before translating, or select just the areas you need (rectangles or circles). Download or copy the result as a new image.

• Whole PDFs
In the browser's built-in PDF viewer, right-click without selecting text to translate the entire document in a page next to it. Translations are laid over the original positions, so layout, figures and table lines stay in place. Compare side by side, show only the translation or only the original, and save the result as a PDF through the print dialog. You can also drop a local PDF onto the translation page.

• Page regions
Right-click an area of a page to add a translation button to it. Click once to replace the text in that area with its translation, and again to restore the original. Rules are remembered per website.

• Translation panel
A dedicated page with the original on the left and the translation on the right, for longer passages or text from outside the browser. Detect the source language automatically or choose it yourself.

24 interface and target languages: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Turkish, Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Japanese and Korean. Interface, source and target languages are independent settings, and Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu interfaces use a right-to-left layout.

▍Two ways to use it

• Key-free credit translation
A new eligible installation receives a guest trial (claims are limited per network), and signing up with email or Google adds more credits. Credits never expire and are deducted by the amount of text actually translated (1 credit per 500 characters); failed translations are not charged. Once trial credits run out, credit translation requires a subscription, which gives unlimited use (fair use), renews automatically and can be canceled at any time. See the website and your account center for current trial amounts and prices.

• Your own API key
Enter your own OpenAI, DeepSeek or DeepL key. Requests go directly from your browser to that provider, which bills you for usage. Image recognition requires a vision-capable model; DeepL does not offer image recognition or alternative wordings.

▍Privacy

• Content is sent only when you choose to translate it. The extension never scans or uploads pages in the background.
• With your own key, content goes directly to the provider you chose, never through us.
• With credit translation, content passes through our server to a model provider; the server does not store your text, translations or images.
• API keys, wording preferences and saved articles stay on your device and are not synced through your browser account.
• No ads, tracking or third-party analytics. The complete source code is available under GPL-3.0.

Live video captions are an experimental feature, off by default. Speech is recognized on your device by default; if you explicitly choose cloud recognition, audio is sent to your browser's speech recognition service.

▍Known limitations

• Browser-protected pages, such as internal chrome:// pages and the extension stores, cannot be used.
• A single selection can contain up to 6,000 characters; longer selections are truncated with a notice.
• Bilingual reading detects articles from the page structure; forums, social networks and pages behind a login may not be detected completely.
• Scanned and password-protected PDFs are not supported yet.
• Available languages, image recognition and streaming depend on the provider and model you choose.

Requires Chrome or Edge 116 or later.
